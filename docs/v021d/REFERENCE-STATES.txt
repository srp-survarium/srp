NEW SRP ACQUISITION POLICY

recovery.rs is recreated with each coordinator/match generation. It retains a
10-second history, folded membership mask plus at most 1,024 pending membership
events, timestamped CRCs, one outstanding request and at most one full candidate.
With 20 slots and 50 ms sample grid, CRC retention is at most 4,020 slot/time
entries. These limits and donor policy are implementation choices, not recovered
community-server arbitration.

Choose the lowest connected playing slot as reference. After activation, send
bodyless ordered/reliable 0x59 at most once per five seconds. An unanswered
request expires after five seconds; donor departure selects the next playing
slot and discards the old candidate/request. A live but nonresponsive reference
is retried, not silently replaced. Match finish stops acquisition.

0x59 has no request token or requested timestamp. Therefore uploaded state must
be checked on its own merits. Accept only from the requested donor, with a
nonzero 50 ms-aligned time inside retained history and no later than server
time; require parsed mask equal to historical membership, including the donor.
Check populated state boundaries against the static roster. Keep its body
immutable and reject changed bodies for an already cached timestamp. Ignore
unsolicited, stale, future or membership-incompatible replies. Malformed bodies
fail that peer, using the existing server error-isolation path.

A cached candidate is corroborated only if the donor's same-time 0x46 exists,
matches its computed CRC, and has not been revised during retention. Missing
samples are not mismatches. Different reports for one slot/time make that sample
ambiguous, even if the old CRC later reappears. Exact duplicate reports do not
inflate comparison counts. Input accepted at or before a candidate time discards
that candidate and invalidates affected hash evidence, requiring fresh reports.

Same-time CRC reports from slots present in the historical mask are compared;
disagreements are logged. This does not establish an honest majority, prove
physics, or trigger immediate resync. Sustained-disagreement policy, recovery
rate limits and complete replay remain future work.

CLIENT RESTORE CONTRACT NEEDED FOR NEXT STEP

Existing patched disassembly game_world_core::deserialize(buffer_reader&),
VA 0x1cab3b, restores player state then, on first synchronization, seeds input
history for each restored active player at the snapshot timestamp from that
player's m_input (0x1cae1a..0x1cae3b). This is evidence against inventing zero
inputs for populated players. Later accepted changes still need replay.

game_world_core::clear 0x1ca788 clears state history/deactivates players but does
not indiscriminately delete the separate input/event histories. State restore
also updates last-full-sync time at 0x1cad6d..0x1cad79. A correct replay design
must account for this preserved history and the client's age checks; do not
assume 52/53 produces a blank input history in an already running client.
